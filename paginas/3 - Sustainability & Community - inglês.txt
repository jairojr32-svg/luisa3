OUR PHILOSOPHY

Headline:  
Luxury with Purpose
Salar de Uyuni is one of the most extraordinary and breathtaking places on Earth — a landscape of endless beauty, silence, and wonder unlike anywhere else in the world. At Salar Balloons, we believe extraordinary places deserve to be celebrated, protected, and shared with deep respect.
Operating in such a unique natural environment comes with a profound sense of responsibility. From the very beginning, our vision has been clear: to create a world-class luxury experience that exists in harmony with the extraordinary beauty of Salar de Uyuni.
Hot air ballooning offers a truly unique perspective — allowing guests to experience this remarkable landscape from above while preserving the authenticity and natural beauty that make it so special.
Our commitment is to continuously seek thoughtful ways to protect, value, and help preserve the extraordinary beauty of Salar de Uyuni for generations to come.
Because true luxury honors the places that inspire it.

COMMUNITY & SOCIAL IMPACT

Headline:  
Growing Together with the Community

At Salar Balloons, we believe tourism can be a transformative force.

Our vision goes beyond creating unforgettable experiences for travelers — we want to help create opportunities for the people who call this extraordinary region home.

We are deeply committed to generating meaningful local impact through employment, training, partnerships, and long-term investment in the community.

We aim to actively involve local people in our operation, valuing local talent and helping create sustainable economic opportunities through tourism.

––––––––––––––––––

HOW WE SUPPORT THE COMMUNITY

• Supporting Local Artisans  
We proudly value local craftsmanship by sourcing gifts, souvenirs, and selected products from regional artisans whenever possible.

• Local Employment & Training  
We are committed to hiring, training, and empowering local talent — creating opportunities for long-term professional growth.

• Community Inclusion  
We believe local people should also experience the beauty of their own region from above. Through weekly community flights, we aim to provide opportunities for residents to experience balloon flight themselves.

• Strengthening Local Tourism  
By bringing international visibility to Salar de Uyuni and Bolivia, we hope to help create lasting economic opportunities for the region.

• Investing in Local Development  
We will always seek meaningful ways to contribute positively to the communities around us and support initiatives that create real local benefit.
